Our Story

Redefining Automotive Excellence

DriveX Motors was founded in 2018 in Karachi, Pakistan, with a singular vision: to make the world's most extraordinary automobiles accessible to collectors and enthusiasts everywhere.

From our state-of-the-art showroom, we curate a selection of 100+ premium, exotic, and luxury vehicles — from everyday grand tourers to rare limited-edition hypercars worth millions.

Our global delivery network spans 120+ countries. With full documentation, transparent pricing, and dedicated specialists, we've completed over 2,000 successful transactions across six continents.
